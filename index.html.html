Common Student Questions

What are target papers, and how do they help?

Target papers are practice papers made around the topics and question pattern that appear most often in board exams. Solving them regularly helps you find weak areas early, improve speed and walk into the exam with more confidence.

How do I check my Pre-Engineering result?

Open the Result Portal from the menu, enter your roll number and press View Result. Your result card will open, and you can save it as a PDF or print it using the buttons below the card.

How do I save or print my result card?

After your card appears, press Save as PDF. The file will be named RESULT followed by your roll number, so it is easy to find later. You can also press Print Card to print a copy.

Which classes and subjects does Nexus cover?

We are preparing study material for Class 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th and Commerce students. Notes, target papers and guess papers will be uploaded class by class, and each section will appear in the Study Material page.

How can I get the latest updates first?

Join our official WhatsApp channels from the home page. Results, date sheet changes and new PDFs are shared there as soon as they are published.

About Nexus Education

Nexus Education is a student-first learning platform built by Team Nexus. We exist for one purpose: to make board exam preparation clear, organised and stress-free for every student, whether they study in a big city or a small town.

Students today often lose hours searching for scattered PDFs, old papers and result notices. We bring the important things into one place, carefully arranged by class and subject, so you can spend your energy on learning instead of searching.

Our target papers, class notes and past-paper practice are chosen to show you the topics that matter most. Our WhatsApp channels then deliver result announcements, date sheet changes and fresh material the moment it is ready, so you never miss an important update.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Midnight Target Paper?

It is a target paper released shortly before the board exam, often around midnight, to help students revise the most important topics and question patterns in their final hours.

When is it released?

Midnight papers are usually released 1 to 2 days before the board paper, and at most one week before. The exact date and time are announced on our WhatsApp channel.

What is the price?

This is a paid product. The price is announced on our WhatsApp channel before the paper, so you can decide with full information.

Is it exactly the same as the paper?

Midnight papers are prepared to match the board pattern and often contain many closely matching questions. However, no paper can be guaranteed to match exactly. It is a strong part of your preparation, not a replacement for complete study.
